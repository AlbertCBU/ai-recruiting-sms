Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase.

How SMS Enrollment Works

  1. Text START to (478) 276-7549.
  2. You will receive an SMS asking you to confirm enrollment.
  3. Reply Y to confirm that you want to participate.
  4. You will receive confirmation that your SMS enrollment is complete.
