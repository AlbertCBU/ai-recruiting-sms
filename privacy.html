Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Albert Ruiz ("we," "us," or "our") handles information collected through the AI Recruiting Prototype, including its SMS demonstration program.

Information We Collect

For purposes of this recruiting prototype, we may collect information voluntarily provided by participants, including names, phone numbers, SMS messages, recruiting-related responses, and interview availability.

How We Use Information

Information collected through this prototype is used to develop, test, and demonstrate an AI-assisted recruiting communication system. This may include sending recruiting follow-ups, responding to recruiting-related questions, coordinating interview scheduling, and demonstrating automated recruiting workflows.

SMS Communications

Participants may voluntarily opt in to receive SMS messages by following the opt-in instructions provided on our SMS Demo page. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Participants may reply STOP at any time to opt out of SMS messages or HELP for assistance.

Mobile Information and SMS Consent

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service or messaging infrastructure, may be permitted when necessary to provide the SMS service.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Service Providers

We may use service providers to operate and demonstrate the prototype, including communications, automation, hosting, and artificial intelligence service providers. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services.

Data Retention

Information collected during prototype testing may be retained as necessary for development, demonstration, troubleshooting, and compliance purposes and may be deleted when it is no longer needed.

Your Choices

Participation in the SMS demonstration is voluntary. You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the prototype changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or the SMS demonstration may be directed to the operator of the AI Recruiting Prototype.
